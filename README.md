Customer Churn Analysis – Power BI

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn and retention to understand customer behavior and identify the factors associated with customer attrition.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to compare churned and retained customers and generate meaningful business insights.

Business Objective

- Understand customer churn patterns.
- Identify factors associated with customer attrition.
- Analyze customer satisfaction and complaints.
- Compare churned and retained customer behavior.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Microsoft Power BI: Data modeling, DAX measures, data visualization, and dashboard development.

Project Workflow

1. Imported the customer dataset.
2. Cleaned and prepared the data using Excel.
3. Loaded the cleaned data into Power BI.
4. Created KPI cards and interactive visualizations.
5. Analyzed churn and retention patterns.
6. Developed business insights and recommendations.

Key Analysis Areas

- Customer Churn vs Retention
- Customer Satisfaction Score
- Customer Complaints
- Customer Tenure
- Preferred Order Category
- City Tier Analysis
- Days Since Last Order

Key Business Insights

- Customer complaints are an important area to investigate in relation to churn.
- Customer satisfaction levels help identify potential retention challenges.
- Preferred product categories show differences between churned and retained customers.
- Customer tenure provides insights into customer retention patterns.
- City tier analysis helps understand geographical customer distribution.

Business Recommendations

- Improve complaint resolution and customer support.
- Collect customer feedback regularly.
- Improve customer experience in high-churn product categories.
- Introduce targeted customer retention strategies.
- Monitor customer satisfaction and engagement.

Conclusion
